Core screens

  • Executive homepage with revenue pulse, spend pulse, confidence, and immediate actions.
  • Channel intelligence with spend, revenue, blended efficiency, new customer share, and trust deltas.
  • Acquisition truth page showing brand, remarketing, and retention contamination.
  • Campaign and creative audit workspace for specialists and agency control.
  • Measurement trust center comparing platform, GA4, and backend reality.

Design principles

  • No fake certainty.
  • Every number should know its source.
  • Business metrics before ad-platform vanity.
  • Warnings should be first-class UI elements.
  • Leadership view and operator view should live in one system.
