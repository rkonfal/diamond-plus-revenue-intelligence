# Product Blueprint

## Product direction

Diamond Plus Revenue Intelligence should behave like a revenue operating system, not just another dashboard.

It should combine:
- harmonized marketing costs,
- on-site demand and conversion signals,
- order and customer reality from backend systems,
- business-quality layers like margin, returns, cancellations, and confidence.

## Main user roles

### 1. Owner / CEO
Needs:
- one-screen understanding of business health
- clear answer where revenue is coming from
- immediate risks and actions
- trust indicators when measurement is weak

### 2. Head of marketing / growth
Needs:
- channel efficiency
- new vs returning split
- campaign-level truth
- ability to challenge agency reporting

### 3. Channel specialist / operator
Needs:
- drilldown from channel to campaign / creative / audience
- fast identification of waste
- precise next actions

### 4. Finance / operations
Needs:
- tie marketing spend to real orders and contribution
- understand returns, cancellations, and profitability

## Product pillars

### A. Unified revenue truth
Single layer joining:
- ad spend
- sessions / user behavior
- orders
- revenue
- margins
- returns / cancellations
- customer status (new / returning)

### B. Attribution humility
Never pretend all numbers are equally true.
Every major metric should have confidence framing:
- platform-reported
- analytics-observed
- backend-confirmed
- modeled / estimated

### C. Decision-first UX
Every page should answer:
- what happened,
- why it likely happened,
- what to do next,
- how sure we are.

### D. Management + operator duality
Top layer for fast leadership decisions.
Lower layer for specialist drilldown.

## MVP screens

### 1. Executive homepage
Components:
- revenue pulse
- spend pulse
- blended efficiency
- new customer contribution
- measurement confidence strip
- biggest positive / negative change
- immediate actions

### 2. Channel intelligence
Per channel:
- spend
- revenue
- ROAS / PNO
- new customer revenue
- returning customer revenue
- margin contribution
- attribution confidence
- trend vs prior period

### 3. Customer acquisition truth
- new vs returning split
- true acquisition channels
- recycled demand warning
- brand / remarketing contamination warning

### 4. Campaign audit workspace
- campaign ranking
- weak-spend detection
- suspiciously inflated reporting
- exclusions / audience hygiene notes
- tracking quality notes

### 5. Measurement & trust center
- source coverage
- UTM quality
- channel mapping gaps
- unattributed revenue
- platform vs GA4 vs backend deltas

## Non-negotiable features

- CZ / SK split
- date comparison
- business-first metrics before vanity metrics
- warning system for mixed acquisition / retention logic
- ability to export clean views for agency conversations
