# Metric Dictionary

## Top-level management metrics

### Revenue
Backend-confirmed gross or net revenue, explicitly labeled.

### Spend
Paid media spend aggregated across channels.

### Blended ROAS
Backend-confirmed revenue / total marketing spend.

### PNO
Spend / revenue.

### New customer revenue
Revenue from customers whose first confirmed purchase falls within the selected period.

### Returning customer revenue
Revenue from customers with confirmed historical purchase before the selected period.

### Contribution after marketing
Revenue minus marketing spend minus selected business costs.

### Measurement confidence
A scored label showing how trustworthy the current view is.
